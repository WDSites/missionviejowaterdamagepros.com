First check: is your home built on a slab at all?

Slab leaks only happen in homes built on concrete slabs. We cannot say how many Mission Viejo homes sit on one, so check your own. Look for a foundation edge with no crawl space vents or basement door, or ask your insurer or the city for original plans.

Age matters for the pipes inside. According to housing data compiled for the Southern California Association of Governments, about two-thirds of Mission Viejo homes were built between 1970 and 1989. Supply lines, water heaters and fittings from that era have had decades of service, so a slab home of that age deserves a quick look at the water bill each month.

A pressurized supply line under the floor spins the meter and warms the concrete. A cracked drain line leaks only when water runs, and it often leaves a damp, sour smell near the slab edge.

Signs the water is already above the slab

A warm spot on the floor

A hot water line is leaking under the concrete and heating the surface. Tile and hard floors show it first, bare feet notice it fastest.

Running water with fixtures off

A faint hiss or trickle in a quiet house points to a pressurized line. Listen near the water heater and along walls with plumbing.

A water bill that jumps

A sudden rise with no change in habits can mean steady escape. Compare this month to the same month last year.

Wet carpet or damp baseboards

Moisture wicks up from the slab into carpet pad, tack strip and the bottom of drywall. The wall often looks dry above a wet base.

Damp slab edge or lifting flooring

Water that cannot go down pushes sideways and up. Laminate peaks, vinyl planks buckle and wood floors cup and gap.

Safety steps for the first hour

Water and electricity do not mix. If water is near outlets, a panel or appliances, stay out and use the breaker only if you can reach it from dry ground. If you smell gas or see sparks, leave and call 911 or the gas utility before anything else.

Next, shut the water off at the house main, then run one faucet to drain pressure. If the main is stuck or you cannot find it, the utility can help. Mission Viejo is served by four water districts, according to the city of Mission Viejo: El Toro, Moulton Niguel, Santa Margarita and Trabuco Canyon. Which one bills you depends on your address, so find your bill and keep that district's number handy.

Some districts, such as Moulton Niguel according to its own service page, run a 24/7 line for supply problems. Call yours if you cannot isolate the leak. Then call a plumber, and then call us.

The meter test that confirms a hidden leak

A water meter leak check takes about fifteen minutes. Turn off every fixture, ice maker, irrigation timer and appliance that uses water. Read the meter or watch the small leak indicator, usually a triangle or dial. If it keeps moving, water is escaping somewhere between the meter and your fixtures.

A moving meter does not prove the leak is under the slab. It could be a running toilet or an irrigation line. A leak detection company uses acoustic and thermal tools to pinpoint the spot before anyone cuts concrete.

How we dry the floor, subfloor and walls

1

Map the moisture

We use moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera to trace how far the water spread. Concrete moisture tests tell us how wet the slab itself is before flooring goes back.

2

Remove what cannot dry

Carpet pad, soaked baseboards and swollen drywall come out first. Salvageable hard flooring may stay if it can be dried in place.

3

Dry with mats and dehumidification

Drying mats pull moisture from hardwood and subfloor. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry cabinet toe kicks, walls and the slab surface, with readings logged each visit.

4

Confirm dry, then rebuild

We rebuild only after readings match a dry reference and the plumber has finished. For equipment and crew practices, ask any company whether it works to ANSI/IICRC S500, the published water damage standard.

Concrete holds water, so slab drying often runs longer than a wall leak. If water first spread as an open flood, our emergency water removal page covers extraction.

Insurance and slab leaks

Most policies treat sudden, accidental water damage differently from slow leaks. A pipe that bursts is more likely covered than a pinhole that wept for months, and the cost of reaching a hidden pipe is often handled separately from the damage it caused.

The California Department of Insurance says insurers may not cover ensuing damage if you do not take reasonable steps to protect the property, so start mitigation promptly and keep a log of calls and paperwork. Flood is a separate matter, because standard policies exclude it. Ask your insurer what your policy says before you decide.

How do I know if I have a slab leak?

Does my Mission Viejo home have a slab?

Do you fix the pipe?

No. A plumber or leak detection company finds and repairs the line, often by breaking the slab or re-routing pipe. We dry and restore the flooring, subfloor materials, baseboards and walls the water reached.

My floor is wet but the meter is still. What now?

A still meter after a test can mean a drain line leak, an old leak or condensation. We can map the moisture, and the plumber can run a pressure test. Do not assume the water is gone.
