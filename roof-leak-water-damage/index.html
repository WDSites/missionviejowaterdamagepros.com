Water coming through the roof is an interior problem within hours

Roof leak water damage in Mission Viejo, CA is what happens after rain gets past shingles, tile, flashing, a vent boot or a skylight curb and soaks what sits below: attic insulation, ceiling drywall, wall cavities and anything hanging from the ceiling. The roof is only where it started.

If water is dripping right now, call our 24/7 line and keep reading while you wait. Move people and valuables out from under the wet spot, set a bucket, and stay out of any room where the ceiling is bulging. Dispatch is same-day response when available, with no promised arrival time.

How rain finds its way in, and why you often see it late

Southern California rain is seasonal. NOAA climate normals for the nearest coastal station, Laguna Beach, put the annual average near 13.6 inches, with most of it from November through April and almost none in July and August. That long dry gap lets a cracked flashing seam or a slipped tile sit unnoticed until the first real storm. Walk the attic after the first rain of the season.

Storms here can also arrive in clusters. According to the National Weather Service, storms in early February 2024 dropped 4 to 10 inches on Orange County over several days, and two waves of heavy rain in January 2023 caused widespread flooding across the county. Days of steady rain push water sideways under roofing, so a roof that stayed dry in a short shower can leak in a long one.

The roof type changes where to look. Tile can shed most rain yet leak at cracked tiles, worn underlayment and valleys. Composition shingle can lift or crack at nail lines and around penetrations. Either way the entry point is often far from the stain, because water runs along the underside of the decking before it drops.

What the ceiling and attic are telling you

A brown or tan ring on the ceiling

Water has passed through the drywall and dried at its edge. Rings that grow after each rain mean an active leak above, not an old one.

Paint bubbling or a soft bulge

Water is pooled behind the drywall face. Pressing it can burst the bubble, so leave it and call.

A sagging ceiling

Wet drywall is heavy and the screws can pull through. Stay out from under it and keep the room closed.

Dark, flattened attic insulation

Soaked insulation holds water against the drywall for days. Wet batts lose their air gaps and keep feeding moisture downward.

Water in a light fixture or fan

Water has followed the wiring or the cutout. Do not use that switch until the circuit is made safe.

Roof leak safety: what to do in the first hour

Your goal is to keep people safe and slow the spread. You are not trying to fix the roof. Do not climb onto a wet roof, and do not walk across attic joists in the dark, because the only thing between you and the ceiling below is drywall.

  • If water is near a light fixture, outlet or panel, turn off that circuit at the breaker only if you can reach the panel without standing in water. Otherwise stay out and call your electric utility or 911.
  • Keep everyone out from under a bulging or sagging ceiling. A relieved ceiling can drop wet drywall and insulation at once.
  • Put a bucket or tub under drips and move furniture, rugs and electronics out of the path.
  • Take photos and short video of the stain, the drips and the wet attic before anything is moved.
  • Call a roofer for a tarp or temporary repair and call our 24/7 line for the interior, in either order.

If you smell gas or see sparks, leave the house and call 911 first. Everything else can wait.

How the crew dries a leak from the roofline down

1

Map the wet area

A moisture meter and a thermal imaging camera trace the water from the stain back along the ceiling, wall tops and attic to find where it actually spread.

2

Pull what cannot dry

Soaked insulation comes out in bags. Sagging or waterlogged drywall is cut back to dry framing, with plastic sheeting up so dust stays in the room.

3

Dry the structure

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers go in, and attic and cavity airflow is set up where the wet is. Readings are logged daily, usually over several days.

4

Confirm dry, then hand off

Readings must reach the dry standard before drywall goes back. Ask whether your crew works to ANSI/IICRC S500, the published standard for water damage restoration.

Wet wall framing is a separate drying job from the ceiling, and we cover it on our structural drying page. Hidden pockets of wet insulation behind a finished ceiling are the usual reason a stain comes back.

According to the Southern California Association of Governments, using American Community Survey data, about two-thirds of Mission Viejo homes were built between 1970 and 1989. Roofs on those homes have usually been replaced, patched or both, and the old penetrations, vent pipes and skylights are where repair seams tend to fail. If your roof has been patched, look at those spots first.

What drives the cost of a roof leak cleanup

How much got wet

A ceiling stain over one room is a small job. Water that ran into walls and across several rooms means more removal and more equipment.

Attic insulation

Blown-in insulation soaked across a large attic costs more to remove than a few wet batts.

Time before drying began

A leak found the same day dries differently from one that has soaked for weeks, where drywall and framing may need replacing.

Rebuild scope

Ceiling texture, paint, crown and lighting repair are priced separately from drying.

Insurance and roof leak water damage

Homeowners policies often treat a storm that opens a sudden hole in the roof differently from a leak that has dripped for months. Sudden and accidental interior damage is often covered, while wear, neglect and long-term seepage often are not, and the roof repair itself may be handled differently from the ceiling. Check your policy and ask your insurer what applies.

The California Department of Insurance notes that a policy may not cover ensuing damage if you did not take reasonable steps to protect the property. A tarp and fast drying are those steps. Keep your roofer's invoice, your photos and our drying log together. Flood coverage is separate, and standard homeowners policies do not cover flood.

Mistakes that make a roof leak worse

  • Waiting for a stain to dry before calling anyone. It dries to a ring and the insulation above stays wet.
  • Painting over the stain. Primer hides it while the drywall and attic stay damp.
  • Leaving wet insulation in place because the ceiling looks fine.
  • Drying the room before the roof is tarped or sealed.
  • Hiring a door-knocker after a storm. The state licensing board warns against rushing into repairs, so verify a contractor's license number first.

Roof leak questions

Is emergency roof leak water damage cleanup available in Mission Viejo?

Yes. Our line is open 24/7 and we offer same-day response when available, including nights and weekends. We do not promise an arrival time, but the dispatcher will tell you what to do while you wait: bucket, photos, keep people out from under a sagging ceiling.

My ceiling is bulging with water. Should I poke a hole in it?

Not unless a professional tells you to. Draining a bulge can relieve weight, but it also lets wet insulation and drywall fall. Place a bucket, clear the room and call.

Do you fix the roof?

No. A roofer finds and repairs the entry point and tarps it. We dry and repair the inside. Call a roofer for the tarp and us for the drying.

Is it safe to use a light fixture that got wet?

No. Leave the switch off and, if you can safely reach the panel, turn off that circuit. If water is pooled near the panel or you see sparks, stay out and call 911 or your utility.

Will my insurance cover a roof leak?

It depends on your policy and on whether the damage was sudden or gradual. Ask your insurer before you sign off on anything, and keep your photos and receipts.
