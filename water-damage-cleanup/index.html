Once the water stops, the cleanup begins

A stopped leak does not mean a finished problem. Wet drywall, soaked pad, swollen trim and damp insulation are still in your home, and some of it may carry contamination. Water damage cleanup is the removal of those materials, followed by sanitizing and deodorizing the space. Our line is open 24/7 for Mission Viejo, with same-day response when available.

Until the crew arrives, keep the main water valve closed, leave power off to wet rooms unless the panel is dry and reachable, and lift valuables out. Keep children and pets out of any water that came from a toilet, drain or outside. If you suspect sewage, do not touch it with bare hands.

Water categories decide what happens next

Industry practice, described in the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, sorts water into three categories by how contaminated it is. The category sets the safety gear, the sanitizing steps and what can be kept. Water that starts clean can turn worse the longer it sits, so time is part of the decision.

What the cleanup looks like when the crew is on site

1

Protect and contain

Crews set plastic sheeting and, for contaminated water, negative air containment with HEPA air scrubbers so dust and spores stay in the work area.

2

Remove debris and ruined materials

Wet drywall, insulation, pad and trim are cut out and bagged. Cuts are made above the water line with a margin so clean material is not left wet.

3

Clean and sanitize

Surfaces and framing are wiped and treated with a cleaner matched to the water category. Contaminated areas get disinfectant, applied to every exposed surface.

4

Deodorize and verify

Odor is treated at the source, not covered up. Air scrubbing, thermal fogging or hydroxyl units are used where needed, and moisture meters confirm the structure is drying.

What lingers after the water leaves

A sour or earthy odor

Damp pad, insulation or drywall is still holding moisture. Smell fades only after the source material is removed.

Stains that bleed through paint

Tannin and minerals leach out of wet drywall and wood. Painting over them rarely holds.

Dust that returns after cleanup

Fine dust from cut drywall settles on every surface. HEPA air scrubbers and damp wiping capture it.

Spotty mold on baseboards or corners

Mold starts where moisture lingers. Fix the water problem first, then see mold remediation if growth has begun.

When storm water is the source

The National Weather Service recorded storms on January 14 to 16, 2023 that caused widespread flooding in Orange County, and on February 4 to 8, 2024 Orange County was hit with 4 to 10 inches of rain. Water that enters from outside often carries soil and runoff, which pushes it into the contaminated end of the scale. If rain came through a door, treat the wet area as suspect and keep it off limits.

Free sandbags are available around the clock at city sites such as Alicia Park and Gilleran Park, so stock up before the next system. After a storm, clear gutters, downspouts and low yard areas so water has somewhere to go. If water entered the house anyway, emergency water removal comes first, then cleanup.

Older homes need a pause before demolition

Regional housing data drawn from Census surveys shows about 79 percent of Mission Viejo homes were built before 1990. Older drywall joint compound, flooring and textured ceilings may contain materials that need testing before they are cut or sanded. Ask the company whether it tests suspect materials before demolition. The CSLB lists asbestos abatement as its own licensed specialty, which tells you the work is regulated.

What drives the cost of cleanup

Water category

Category 3 requires protective gear, containment and disinfection, which clean-water jobs do not.

Volume of material removed

More drywall, insulation, pad and flooring means more labor, more disposal and more hauling.

Access and containment

Tight spaces, second floors and areas that need sealed barriers add setup time.

Odor and sanitizing needs

Persistent odors call for air scrubbers, fogging or hydroxyl units, which add equipment days.

Rebuild scope

Everything removed has to be replaced, so the rebuild list grows with the demolition list.

Insurance, contracts and who to hire

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered by homeowners policies, and gradual leaks often are not. The California Department of Insurance says standard homeowners coverage does not include flood, so flood claims go to NFIP if you carry that coverage. Check your policy, ask your insurer, and keep a log of every call.

On the contractor side, state rules from the Contractors State License Board say a down payment is capped by law, you get a three-day right to cancel and the contract must show a licence number. Check the number on the CSLB site and be cautious of door-to-door offers after storms. The state also advises against rushing into repairs. Ask whether your repairs need a permit from the city's Building Services staff.

Questions about water damage cleanup in Mission Viejo

Do you handle water damage cleanup emergencies at night or on weekends in Mission Viejo?

Yes. The line is open 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays, with same-day response when available. We do not promise a specific arrival time. Tell dispatch whether the water was clean, from a drain or from outside so the right crew goes out.

What is the difference between Category 1, 2 and 3 water?

Category 1 is clean water from a supply line or similar source. Category 2 is gray water from appliances or sumps. Category 3 is grossly contaminated, such as sewage or floodwater. The category decides the gear, the sanitizing and what materials are removed.

Can my drywall be saved after a leak?

Sometimes. If the water was clean, the wall dried quickly and the cavity was opened or treated, drywall can stay. If the water was gray or black, or the wall sat wet for days, it is generally removed.

Why does carpet pad almost always get thrown out?

Foam pad holds contaminants and is cheap to replace compared with the labor of cleaning it. Even clean water can leave minerals and bacteria in it. The carpet above it can sometimes be saved.

Will the smell go away after cleanup?

Usually, once the source material is removed. Odor is treated with air scrubbers, fogging or hydroxyl units where needed. If the smell returns, moisture is still in the structure and a meter check is the next step.

Is sewage cleanup different?

Yes. Sewage is Category 3. Crews use protective gear, contain the area, remove porous materials and disinfect. Stay out, keep pets and children away, and call the utility or 911 first if there is a gas or electrical hazard.

Should I do the cleanup myself to save money?

Small clean-water spills on hard floors are fine for a homeowner. Anything wider, any gray or black water, or any wet drywall deserves a trained crew with meters and proper disposal.
