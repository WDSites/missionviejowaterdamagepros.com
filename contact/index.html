Contact Mission Viejo Water Damage Pros

The fastest way to reach us is the 24/7 phone line. Use this page to get your details ready and to see what happens once you call.

How to reach us

For anything active, call the 24/7 phone line shown on this page. It is answered day and night, including weekends and holidays, because wet drywall and standing water do not wait for Monday.

Our postal address is 24001 Via Fabricante, Mission Viejo, CA 92691. We dispatch to contractors who work in the field, so please call before driving over. For non-urgent questions you can write to contact@missionviejowaterdamagepros.com, but if water is spreading, the phone is quicker than email.

Before you call: safety first

Some situations need a different call before ours. If water is near an electrical panel, outlets or a light fixture that is dripping, stay out of the room and shut off power only if you can do it safely, from a dry spot. If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 or your gas utility from outside.

Raw sewage coming up through a drain is a health matter. Keep people and pets away from it, and do not try to wipe it up yourself. Once everyone is safe, call us.

What to have ready

You can call with nothing in hand, but a minute of preparation makes the call shorter and the dispatch more accurate.

  • Photos or a short video of the damage, taken before you move anything
  • The source of the water, if you know it: pipe, appliance, roof, drain, rain
  • Whether the source is stopped and where your main water shutoff valve is
  • Whether the power is on, off or partly off in the affected area
  • Which rooms and floors are wet, and roughly how large the wet area is
  • Your insurance company name and policy number, if you have them handy

What happens on the call

1

We ask about the water

A dispatcher asks where the water came from, what is wet and whether anyone is in danger. Honest answers matter more than tidy ones.

2

We confirm the address

We check that your address falls inside the Mission Viejo area or one of the five nearby cities we serve.

3

We pass the job to a contractor

Your details go to a licensed and insured local restoration contractor. Same-day response when available, but we cannot promise an arrival time.

4

The contractor explains scope and price

The contractor assesses the property and explains scope and price before work starts. You decide whether to go ahead.

While you wait

  • Stop the source: close the fixture valve or the main water valve.
  • Cut power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel safely.
  • Lift small valuables, books, rugs and electronics off wet floors.
  • Open cabinet doors under a sink or beside a wet wall so air can reach them.
  • Keep people and pets out of any water that may be contaminated.
  • Write down the time you found the damage and what you did. Your insurer may ask, and the California Department of Insurance advises keeping a log of calls and correspondence.

Taking reasonable steps to protect the property matters. According to the California Department of Insurance, a policy may not cover further damage if you did not act to secure the home. Check your own policy for the details.

Local contacts worth saving

Not every water problem starts inside your walls. Moulton Niguel Water District publishes a 24/7 line on its website. If the leak is on the supply side of your meter, or you cannot tell whether it is, that is a call to make. The city lists four water providers for Mission Viejo, so check which one bills you before an emergency.

The city of Mission Viejo Public Works department maintains the drainage and flood control systems. If a street catch basin is clogged and water is backing toward your garage or front door, report it to the city instead of waiting for the next rain.

Before a storm, the city offers free sandbags around the clock at seven city sites, including Alicia Park, Gilleran Park and the Civic Center, with a limit of 10 per resident. Sandbags help protect a doorway from outside water. They do not dry anything, so call us once water is inside.

Contact questions

Is the phone line really open 24 hours?

Yes. The line is answered day and night, every day of the year. We say same-day response when available, but no one can promise an arrival time.

Can I send photos by email?

You can email contact@missionviejowaterdamagepros.com with photos, but please call first when water is active. A contractor can start planning from your call, while an email may sit until someone reads it.

Do you accept walk-ins at your address?

Please do not drive over without calling. The address, 24001 Via Fabricante in Mission Viejo, is our postal address, and the work happens at customers' homes through local contractors.

What if I am outside Mission Viejo?

Call anyway. We dispatch to Lake Forest, Laguna Niguel, Rancho Santa Margarita, Laguna Hills and San Juan Capistrano as well, and the service area page explains how coverage works.
